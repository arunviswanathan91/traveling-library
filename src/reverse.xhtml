Convert Zotero citations to EndNote

This experimental converter saves a separate DOCX with EndNote citation fields and an embedded traveling library. It uses the reference metadata saved in the document. Refresh and save the source with Zotero first if you recently edited its references.

Open the converted copy in desktop Word with EndNote installed. Export its Traveling Library to a new EndNote library, choose your EndNote style, and test Update Citations and Bibliography and Edit & Manage Citation(s).

New EndNote record identifiers are generated. Existing .enl records are not automatically linked. The current text stays visible until EndNote formats it; formatting and numbering may change.

EndNote desktop recognition has not been tested here. Keep the original document and do not unformat the converted copy before testing.
