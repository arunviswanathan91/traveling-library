A separate DOCX will be saved. Current citation and bibliography text remains visible until you open the copy in desktop Word and click Zotero → Refresh. Refresh may change numbering, ordering and formatting.

This first version supports in-text styles. Unknown citation settings, ambiguous references, mixed Zotero/EndNote documents and citations inside tracked changes stop conversion. Save and close the source document in Word before continuing.

The converter has automated tests; Word desktop Refresh and Add/Edit Citation still require confirmation on your computer.
